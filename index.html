Melanoma Detection (Classical CV)

Fully automatic segmentation and classification of dermoscopic skin lesions (benign vs. melanoma) using only classical computer vision, no deep learning. An ensemble of Otsu, K-means and watershed segmentation selected by IoU, then 40 shape, color and texture (LBP/GLCM) features classified with SVM under 5-fold cross-validation. 0.735 mean IoU, 90.2% accuracy, 84% melanoma sensitivity on the ISIC 2018 dataset.
